# Superseded model roadmap

The earlier Trisakti roadmap's proposed 72B/216B sizes, merged checkpoints, training estimates, and dataset collection ideas were not validated against this PC or model licenses. They are superseded by the hardware-tested, license-tracked Brahma V1 work in [`models/brahma/README.md`](models/brahma/README.md).

Brahma V1 is planned as a CodeVaa fine-tune of Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct (9B actual parameters), not a model trained from scratch. No unsupported quality, schedule, cost, or benchmark claims from the old roadmap carry forward.
